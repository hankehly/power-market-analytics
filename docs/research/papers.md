# Papers

External papers cited by the research docs — links only, nothing copied into
the repo. One row per paper: what it is, the PDF, and which research doc
cites it. Add a row only when a research doc cites the paper.

| Paper | PDF | Cited by |
|---|---|---|
| S. Arora, J. W. Taylor, *Short-term Forecasting of Anomalous Load Using Rule-based Triple Seasonal Methods*, IEEE Transactions on Power Systems (2013); GB half-hourly load 2001–2009. Rules 1–4 pick, for each special day, the past special day to lag to: the same special day the previous year (Rule 1), additionally the same weekday (Rule 2) or weekday/weekend class (Rule 3), with the Christmas-period "proximity days" split into bridging (a single working day between a weekend and a holiday) and non-bridging classes; falls back to Rule 1 when no match exists. | [arXiv:1409.2027](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.2027) (authors' accepted version) | The `dim_date` prior-year reference-date design (2026-08-28) — precedent for same-holiday matching and for treating bridge days as their own class |
| M. Borunda, L. Conde-López, G. Ruiz-Chavarría, G. Lopez Lopez, V. M. Alvarado, E. de J. Carrera Avendaño, *Advances in Similar Day Methods for Short-Term Load Forecasting for Power Systems* (review), Forecasting 8(2):32, 2026, CC BY. Survey of similar-day selection criteria (calendar, season, weather, recency), holiday handling and the failure of classical similar-day selection on bridge holidays. | [doi:10.3390/forecast8020032](https://doi.org/10.3390/forecast8020032) · [PDF](https://www.mdpi.com/2571-9394/8/2/32/pdf) | Same design — survey of similar-day selection and its bridge-day limits |
| N. Rubattu, G. Maroni, G. Corani, *Electricity Load and Peak Forecasting: Feature Engineering, Probabilistic LightGBM and Temporal Hierarchies*, ECML-PKDD 2023 AALTD workshop (BigDEAL Challenge 2022 entry). LightGBM load model whose calendar block includes `Holiday`, `Holiday name`, `Weekend`, `Week of month`, `Season`, `Day of year`, `Days since last / until next holiday`. | [PDF (workshop site)](https://ecml-aaltd.github.io/aaltd2023/papers/Electricity%20Load%20and%20Peak%20Forecasting_%20Feature%20Engineering,%20Probabilistic%20LightGBM%20and%20Temporal%20Hierarchies.pdf) | Same design — precedent for holiday-proximity features in a GBM instead of a comparable-day mapping |
| J. López, M. Ceballos, et al., *Classification of Special Days in Short-Term Load Forecasting: The Spanish Case Study*, Energies 12(7):1253, 2019, CC BY. Regression STLF for Spain with 53 day-type dummies: Easter, national holidays split by weekday (weekend / Monday / Tue–Fri), and **days adjacent to holidays as their own variables** — before (Tue–Fri; Monday) and after (Mon–Thu; Friday) — noting the profile is most affected when the day sits between the weekend and the holiday. | [doi:10.3390/en12071253](https://doi.org/10.3390/en12071253) · [PDF](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/12/7/1253/pdf) | Same design — regression precedent: no comparable-day mapping, adjacency encoded as features |
| M. Zimmermann, F. Ziel, *Efficient mid-term forecasting of hourly electricity load using generalized additive models*, arXiv:2405.17070 (2024). GAM with per-holiday effects; fixed-date holidays scaled by a weekday impact factor (zero on a Sunday, maximal Tue–Thu) and every holiday "bridged" from midday of the preceding day to midday of the following day. | [arXiv:2405.17070](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.17070) | Same design — effect-window precedent for the day before / after a holiday |
| 森田圭・真鍋勇介・加藤丈佳・舟橋俊久・鈴置保雄, 「複数の簡易的なデータ参照方法の組み合わせによる翌日電力需要予測の検討」 (*A Study of Electricity Demand Forecast with Combination of Several Simplified Data Reference Methods*), エネルギー・資源 Vol. 38 No. 3, 2017. Day-ahead household-aggregate demand from simple reference days (day type / temperature criteria), combined; notes that 特異日 (お盆・GW・正月) should be forecast from 過去の特異日の傾向（前年の同日値など）. | [J-STAGE article](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjser/38/3/38_1/_article/-char/ja/) · [PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjser/38/3/38_1/_pdf/-char/en) | Same design — Japanese precedent for a same-day-previous-year reference on 特異日 |
