| S. Arora, J. W. Taylor, *Short-term Forecasting of Anomalous Load Using Rule-based Triple Seasonal Methods*, IEEE Transactions on Power Systems (2013); GB half-hourly load 2001–2009. | 森田圭・真鍋勇介・加藤丈佳・舟橋俊久・鈴置保雄, 「複数の簡易的なデータ参照方法の組み合わせによる翌日電力需要予測の検討」 (*A Study of Electricity Demand Forecast with Combination of Several Simplified Data Reference Methods*), エネルギー・資源 Vol. 38 No. 3, 2017. | R.-J. Park, K.-B. Song, B.-S. Its §2.2 is the conventional similar-day baseline the paper's DQN selector is compared against: a weighted Euclidean distance over days apart, the 24-h temperature profile, sun irradiation and rainfall (Eq. 1), whose weights are fitted by least squares so that α·WED + β predicts the target/past-day load difference (Eq. 2) — the mean per-hour absolute relative difference of the two load curves (Eq. 3); the smallest-distance past days are the similar days, the past-day range is chosen by trial and error, and the after-the-fact quality of a selection is 1 / (1 + that difference) (EDS, Eq. 4). | 伊勢淳治・藤崎敬介 (新日本製鐵, now 日本製鉄), 「需要予測装置、方法、及びコンピュータ読み取り可能な記憶媒体」 (*Demand forecasting apparatus, method and computer-readable storage medium*), 特許第4448226号, JP 4448226 B2 issued 2010-04-07; filed 2000-03-07 as 特願2000-062209, published as 特開2001-249916. Next-day maximum electricity demand by multiple regression (polynomial inputs, least squares) over past demand, weather and a graded 休日度合い in place of a 0/1 holiday flag: 第1種 by calendar (日曜・祝日 1.0, 土曜 0.8, 月〜金 0); 第2種 for the 特異期間 年末年始・ゴールデンウィーク・お盆 (first day 0.8, other days 1.0); 第3種 for working days sandwiched between off days (飛び石連休の中日, one day, 0.5; 二飛び石連休の中日, two days, 0.3). Each day takes the largest of the three. The 15-day pattern D ± 7 enters the model compressed by a bottleneck neural network, and 特異日 forecasts get a per-date correction factor.
